Feature | var (Variable) | const (Constant)
| --- | --- | --- |
Mutability | Mutable: Can be reassigned after declaration. | Immutable: Cannot be changed once set.
Assignment | Can be assigned at Runtime. | Must be assigned at Compile-time.
Memory | Allocated memory at runtime. | Usually handled as a literal value by the compiler.
Types | "Can be any type (structs, arrays, etc.)." | "Only basic types (boolean, numeric, string)."
Short-hand | Supports := inside functions. | Does not support :=.
